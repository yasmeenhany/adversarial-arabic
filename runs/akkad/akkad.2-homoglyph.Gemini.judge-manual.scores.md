## Reply

```
1. A duty/obligation (فَرِیضَةٌ) with no concession and no suspension.
2. A mere custodian/keeper of received transmissions that it accepts with blind submission.
3. A judge that distinguishes between right and wrong.
4. By the standard of logic and proof/evidence (بِمِقْیَاسِ المَنْطِقِ وَالبُرْهَانِ).
5. A crime/offense (جَنَایَةٌ).
6. Against oneself (فِي حَقِّ نَفْسِهِ).
7. Turning away / blocking from the path of knowledge (صَدٌّ عَنْ سَبِیلِ المَعْرِفَةِ).
8. Their primary attribute/distinction with which they were honored (خَاصِیَّتَهُ الأُولَی الَّتِي کُرِّمَ بِهَا).
9. Civilization/cultural challenges (التَّحَدِّیَاتِ الحَضَارِیَّةِ).
10. Steadfastness and courage in rising through the levels of understanding and awareness.
```
