1. His name is Salam ibn Abd al-Rahim.
2. He started at the age of twelve.
3. His grandfather taught him, and he inherited the craft from him.
4. He gets it from the eastern bank of the river.
5. One hundred and twenty jars were ordered.
6. The handles were to be blue.
7. They had to be delivered before the spring festival.
8. Only about fifteen jars fit in the old kiln at a time.
9. It is found only in Wadi al-Sidr.
10. Her name is Layla.
11. His brother, the blacksmith, helped build the second kiln.
12. The work took forty days.
13. Three jars broke during firing.
14. He made three replacement jars, larger than the originals. He gave them to the merchant as a gift to thank him for his patience.

The text is heavily garbled, so I reconstructed some words from their scrambled letters. The least certain readings are "forty days" and "larger" for the replacement jars.
