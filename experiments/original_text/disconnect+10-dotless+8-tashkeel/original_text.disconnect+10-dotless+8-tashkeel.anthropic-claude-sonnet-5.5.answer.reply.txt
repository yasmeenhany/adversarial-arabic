1. Salim ibn Abd al-Rahim (سالم بن عبد الرحيم).
2. He started at eighteen.
3. His grandfather taught him.
4. He gets it from the eastern bank of the river.
5. 120 jars were ordered.
6. The handles were to be blue.
7. The jars had to be delivered before the spring festival.
8. About 15 jars fit in the old kiln at one time.
9. The rare stone is found only in Wadi al-Sidr.
10. His daughter is called Layla.
11. His neighbour the blacksmith helped build the second kiln. The word is partly garbled, so this reading is less certain.
12. The work took forty days.
13. Three jars broke during firing.
14. He made three replacement jars, larger than the originals, and gave them to the merchant as a gift to thank him for his patience.
