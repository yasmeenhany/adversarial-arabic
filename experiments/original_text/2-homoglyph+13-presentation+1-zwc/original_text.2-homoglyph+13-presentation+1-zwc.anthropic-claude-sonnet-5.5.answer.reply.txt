1. Salim bin Abd al-Rahim.
2. At twelve years old.
3. His grandfather taught him.
4. From the bank of the eastern river.
5. 120 jars.
6. Blue.
7. Before the Spring Festival.
8. Fifteen jars.
9. In Wadi al-Sidr.
10. Layla.
11. His neighbor, the blacksmith, helped build the second kiln.
12. Forty days of continuous work.
13. Three jars broke.
14. He made three larger jars to replace them and gave them to the merchant to thank him for his patience.
