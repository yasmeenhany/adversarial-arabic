1. Salim ibn Abd al-Rahman (سالم بن عبد الرحمن).
2. The age word is partly garbled. It seems to say he started at twelve ("الثانية عشرة").
3. The word naming his teacher is garbled. It seems to be an older man, perhaps his employer or master (مستخدمه), who passed the craft on to him.
4. From the bank of the river.
5. 120 jars ("مئة وعشرين").
6. Blue (زرقاء).
7. Before the Spring festival ("قبل حلول عيد الربيع").
8. Fifteen jars at a time.
9. Only in Wadi al-Sudur (وادي السدور).
10. Layali (ليالي).
11. The blacksmith (الحداد) helped, and Layali went to the valley to fetch the stones. This sentence is partly garbled.
12. About forty days of continuous work. The number word is garbled.
13. Three jars.
14. He made three larger jars to replace them and gave them to the merchant as thanks for his patience.
