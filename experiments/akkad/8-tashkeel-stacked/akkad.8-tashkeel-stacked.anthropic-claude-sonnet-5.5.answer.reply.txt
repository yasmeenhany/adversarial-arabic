1. Thinking is an obligation (*farīḍa*). There is no license to neglect it and no suspending it.
2. The human mind was not created to be a trustee of received transmissions, accepted through blind submission.
3. It was created to be a judge that distinguishes right from wrong.
4. By the standard of logic and proof.
5. A crime (*jināya*).
6. Against oneself, before it becomes a violation of other people's rights.
7. Obstructing the path to knowledge (*ṣadd ʿan sabīl al-maʿrifa*).
8. They lose their first distinctive quality, the one for which they were honored.
9. The civilizational challenges.
10. Steadfastness and courage in rising through the levels of understanding and awareness.
