1. Thinking is a duty (فريضة) that allows no exemption, so it must never be suspended.
2. It was not created to be a trustee over transmitted beliefs that it accepts blindly and without question.
3. It was created to be a judge that distinguishes right from wrong.
4. It should measure things by the standard of logic and proof.
5. The author calls it a crime (جناية).
6. It is committed first against oneself, before it wrongs the rights of others.
7. The author calls it a barrier (صدّ) against the path of knowledge.
8. The person loses their primary distinguishing quality, the one for which they were honored, namely the mind.
9. The person becomes unable to face the challenges of civilization.
10. The author says it is asked for steadfastness and courage in rising through the levels of understanding and awareness.
