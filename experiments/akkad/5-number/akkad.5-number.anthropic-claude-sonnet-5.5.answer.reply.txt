1. Thinking is a "فَرِيضَةٌ" (an obligation) that allows no exemption and no suspension.
2. It was not created to be a trustee over transmitted material that people accept through blind submission.
3. It was created to be a judge that distinguishes right from wrong.
4. By the measure of logic and proof (المنطق والبرهان).
5. The author calls it a "جَنَايَةٌ" (a crime or offense) and also a wronging of others' rights.
6. It is committed first against oneself, before it wrongs others.
7. The author calls it "صَدٌّ عَنْ سَبِيلِ المَعْرِفَةِ" (a barring of the path to knowledge).
8. The person loses the first distinguishing quality, reason, with which humans were honored.
9. The person becomes unable to face the challenges of civilization.
10. Steadfastness and courage (صمودًا وشجاعة) in rising through the levels of understanding and awareness.

The text has a number of grammatical errors, such as "العقل البشريون" and "الذين يسلم". My answers follow the evident intended meaning.
