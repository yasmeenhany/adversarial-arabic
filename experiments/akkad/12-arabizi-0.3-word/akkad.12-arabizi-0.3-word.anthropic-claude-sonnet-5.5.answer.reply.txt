1. Thinking is an obligation (فريضة). It allows no concession and no suspension.
2. The human mind was not created to be a trustee or keeper of transmitted things received through blind submission and acceptance.
3. It was created to be a judge (حَكَم) who distinguishes right from wrong.
4. It should measure things by the standard of logic and proof.
5. The author calls it a crime (جناية).
6. It is committed first against oneself, before it wrongs others.
7. The author calls it a barrier (sadd) on the road to knowledge.
8. That person loses the mind's first characteristic, the one for which humans were honored.
9. That person becomes unable to face the challenges of civilization.
10. The human soul is asked for steadfastness and courage in climbing the ranks of understanding and awareness.
