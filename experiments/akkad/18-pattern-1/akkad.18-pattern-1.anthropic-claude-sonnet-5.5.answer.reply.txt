1. Thinking is an obligation. It allows no exemption, and nothing can suspend it.
2. The human mind was not created to be a blind follower, accepting transmitted reports passively and without sight.
3. It was created to be a judge, one that distinguishes right from wrong.
4. It should measure things by the yardstick of logical reasoning and proofs.
5. The author calls it a crime (*ajnā'a*).
6. It is committed first against oneself, before it harms the rights of others.
7. The author calls it a turning away from the paths of knowledge.
8. That person loses the special distinction with which they were honored.
9. They become unable to face the challenges and events that confront them.
10. Steadfastness and courage are demanded, so that the soul can rise in the ranks of understanding and awareness.

The Arabic text is partly garbled, so some of these answers (especially 2, 5 and 9) are my best reading of unclear wording.
