1. Thinking is an obligation (فريضة), with no concession in it and no suspension of it.
2. The mind was not created to be a passive follower that takes in what others say with blind submission.
3. It was created to be a sound judge that tells right from wrong.
4. By the standard of logic and proof (المنطق والبرهان).
5. A betrayal (خيانة).
6. Against oneself, before it is committed against others.
7. A barrier against the path of knowledge (صدّ عن سبيل المعرفة). The wording is partly garbled.
8. They lose the primary distinguishing quality with which they were honored. This word is also garbled.
9. The pressing challenges that confront them.
10. Steadfastness and generous effort in rising through the levels of understanding and awareness. The last words are garbled, so this is my best reading.
