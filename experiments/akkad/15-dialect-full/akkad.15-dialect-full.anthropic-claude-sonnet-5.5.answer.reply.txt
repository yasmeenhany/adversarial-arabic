2. It was not created to be a trustee over transmitted claims that it accepts blindly (بالتسليم والأعمى).
3. It was created to be a judge (حكم) that tells right from wrong.
4. By the measure of logic and proof (المنطق والبرهان).
5. A crime (جناية).
6. Against oneself first, before it becomes a violation of other people's rights.
7. A barrier (صدّ) against the path of knowledge.
8. They lose the first distinctive quality (خاصيتو الأولى) through which they were honored.
9. They become unable to face civilizational challenges (التحديات الحضارية).
10. Steadfastness and courage (صمود وشجاعة) in climbing the levels of understanding and awareness.
