2nn ettafkeer fareeda la ro5sa feeha wala ta3teel laha، wal3a8l elbashari lam yo5la8 leyakoon 2ameenan 3ala mano8oolat yatala88aha bettasleem wal2a3ma، bal 5ole8 leyakoon 7akaman yomayyez bayn essawab wal5ata2، waya8ees el2omoor beme8yas elmante8 walborhan. 2nn etta5allee 3an ettafkeer elmonsef elmontag hoo ganaya yartakeboha elmar2 fee 7a88 nafseh 8abl 2an takoon hadman le7o8oo8 el2a5areen.

wakoll mo7awala le7agr el3a8l 2aw man3eh men ennazar fee 7a8a2e8 elkawn wal7aya hi sadd 3an sabeel elma3refa. 2nn el2ensan elzee yosallem 3a8lah leghayreh yaf8ed 5aseyyatah el2oola eltee korrem beha، wayaghdoo 3agezan 3an mowagaha etta7addeyat el7adareyya eltee tatlob men ennafs el2ensaneyya samoodan washaga3a fee arte8a2 marateb elfahm walwa3i.
