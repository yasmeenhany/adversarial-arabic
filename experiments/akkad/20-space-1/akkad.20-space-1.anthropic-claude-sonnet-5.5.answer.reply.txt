1. Thinking is a duty (فريضة): there is no exemption from it and no suspending it.
2. It was not created to be a trustee or guardian of received statements that it accepts blindly and without question.
3. It was created to be a judge (حَكَم) that tells right from wrong.
4. By the standard of logic and rational proof (المنطق والبرهان).
5. A crime (جناية) that a person commits against himself.
6. Against himself, before it becomes a violation of the rights of others.
7. A barrier (صدّ) that blocks the path to knowledge.
8. He loses his original distinguishing quality, the one for which he was honored.
9. He cannot face the challenges of civilization.
10. Steadfastness and courage in rising through the levels of understanding and awareness.
