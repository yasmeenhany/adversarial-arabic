1. Thinking is a duty (فريضة): there is no exemption from it and no suspending it.

2. The human mind was not created to be a trustee (أمين) over transmitted material that it receives through blind submission and acceptance.

3. It was created to be a judge (حَكَم) that distinguishes right from wrong.

4. It should measure things by the standard of logic and demonstration (المنطق والبرهان).

5. The author calls it a crime (جناية).

6. It is committed first against oneself, since a person commits it against his own right before it becomes a violation of the rights of others.

7. The author calls it a barrier (صدّ) blocking the path to knowledge.

8. He loses his first distinctive quality, the one for which he was honored.

9. He becomes unable to face the civilizational challenges.

10. The text says these challenges demand steadfastness and courage from the human soul, in rising through the levels of understanding and awareness.
