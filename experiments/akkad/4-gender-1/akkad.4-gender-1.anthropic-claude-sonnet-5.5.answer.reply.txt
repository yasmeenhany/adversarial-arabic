1. Thinking is a "فَرِيضَة" (an obligation) with no exemption and no suspension.
2. It was not created to be a trustee over transmitted beliefs that it accepts through blind submission.
3. It was created to be a judge that distinguishes right from wrong.
4. By the standard of logic and proof (demonstration).
5. A "جَنَايَة" (a crime or offense).
6. Against oneself, before it is an infringement on the rights of others.
7. "صَدٌّ عَنْ سَبِيلِ المَعْرِفَةِ", a barring of the path to knowledge.
8. They lose their first distinguishing characteristic, the one with which they were honored.
9. They become unable to face the challenges of civilization.
10. Steadfastness and courage in rising through the levels of understanding and awareness.
